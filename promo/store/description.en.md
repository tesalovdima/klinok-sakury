## Full description

**Sakura Blade** is an anime idle RPG where the battle runs itself and you make the decisions: which gear to wear, what to enchant, where to spend gold, and when to awaken.

**Grades and enchanting.** From grade-free starter gear to legendary R: weapons, armor, jewelry and auras. Every item has enchant power, random “Edge” properties and set bonuses. Enchant up to +20, risk a break for power, and rebuy the item if it shatters.

**Auto-battle and bosses.** The heroine fights automatically while you collect gold, scrolls and crystals. Every stage ends with a boss that unlocks a new zone.

**Pets.** Summon companions, raise their stars and complete the collection: every pet grants a permanent bonus.

**Sakura Tower.** 120 floors with unique guardians, weekly rewards and auto-climb. The tower is open from level one.

**Arena.** Real players and fair bots, rating, leagues and daily rewards.

**Arena seasons.** Every week the rating resets, and the final league pays out — from gems to petals and summon tickets.

**World boss.** A shared boss for the whole server: everyone attacks the same enemy, contribution is visible in the top, and rewards are split by damage. A new boss every day.

**Dungeons.** Gold, scrolls, crystals and experience — keys refresh every day.

**10 elemental skills.** Fire, water, wind and earth: petal whirl, moon slash, flame blade, ice spikes, sky thunder, earthquake, blade storm, meteor, war spirit and bloom. Skills unlock with level, level up with skill points and spend mana — with cut-ins, blade glow and full-screen effects.

**Events.** “Sakura Festival” and “Lantern Night”: seasonal currencies, reward tracks and limited shops.

**Weekly quests.** Six goals per week, rewards for each and a big chest for all of them — with progress bars and a countdown.

**Awakening.** Rebirth for sakura petals and permanent bonuses from an 11-node eternal tree.
